# Code of conduct

## Our standard

InkSalah welcomes contributors of every background and level of experience. Project spaces should remain respectful, patient, and focused on improving the software.

Positive participation includes:

- communicating with empathy and clarity;
- respecting religious, cultural, and technical differences;
- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- accepting constructive correction; and
- keeping discussions focused on the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and sexualized conduct or imagery.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that does not follow this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository spaces and when a person is publicly representing the project.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
